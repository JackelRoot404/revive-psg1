# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, accepting
responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable
behavior includes harassment, trolling, insults, sexualized attention,
publishing others' private information, or conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Report conduct issues privately through GitHub. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that
do not align with this Code of Conduct.
